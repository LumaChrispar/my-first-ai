What this is, and what it cannot do

Aster is a tiny experimental neural network written from scratch in C and running entirely on this computer. It was trained on one small local text file. It has no reliable knowledge — it does not know who you are, what year it is, or how anything in the world works, and it cannot look anything up.

It has no web access, no network access, no tools, and no persistent memory. Everything it says is produced by sampling a few hundred thousand numbers. Its replies are frequently wrong, incomplete, or incoherent. Treat every reply below as unverified machine output, never as a fact or as advice.

Where this runs and where your words go

The server is a single process on this machine, bound to the loopback interface (127.0.0.1) only; it refuses to serve any other machine on the network, and this page loads nothing from the internet. Nothing is written to a server-side log.

Your transcript is kept only in this browser's localStorage on this computer and is never transmitted anywhere; when you reload this page it is restored, and Clear erases it.

The model is not trained — no replies can be generated

No checkpoint is loaded on the server, so it has nothing to generate from. Nothing on this page was written by a model, and no example conversation is shown, because any answer you saw here would be invented by the page rather than produced by Aster.
